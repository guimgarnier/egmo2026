After a focused exam session in the morning, the participants enjoyed a well‑deserved break with a variety of afternoon activities.

They could choose between a visit to the Bassin des Lumières, a magic tricks workshop, a pottery workshop, and a dance workshop at the hotel.

They also had the chance to chat with some of our sponsors and learn more about their work. A relaxing and joyful moment to unwind, explore, and share new experiences together.
