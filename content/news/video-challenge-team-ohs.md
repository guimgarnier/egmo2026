{% content_section(title="And the winner is… Team OHS!") %}
As part of EGMO 2026, we launched the [Girls' Maths Video Challenge](/news/video-challenge/), inviting teams of high school girls from around the world to create a short video about a major mathematical breakthrough achieved by a woman scientist.

The jury received many inspiring entries, and after careful deliberation, one stood out for its creativity, the clarity of its mathematical explanation, and the quality of its production.

We are delighted to announce that the winners of the Girls' Maths Video Challenge are **Team OHS**: **Honwell Xing**, **Olivia Lee**, **Audrielle Paige Esma**, and **Connie Jin**. Congratulations to all four of them!
