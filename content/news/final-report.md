{% content_section(title="Key figures") %}

- **15th** edition, first international math olympiad in France since IMO 1983
- **67 teams** from **66 countries**, new participation record
- **260 contestants**, 590 attendees, 46 languages, 9,517 scanned pages
- First-time participants: **Iceland**, **Nigeria**, **Rwanda**
- **1** perfect scorer (Huaijin Lou), **30** gold, **39** silver, **59** bronze medals, **27** honorable mentions
- **Romania**: best European country
- **71% women** among coordinators, a new EGMO record
- Total budget: **€1,009k**
